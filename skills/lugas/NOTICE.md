## Inspiration

Inspired by the `bahasa-indonesia-plain` gist by orepras: https://gist.github.com/orepras/3e1ca8974fc51d11825f142fa6a66635. No text was copied; every sentence, example, word list, and rubric in this skill was written from scratch. The RASA framework name and concept (Ritmis, Akurat, Sarat Makna, Alami) are credited to that source.

The gist carries no license, so nothing from it is redistributed here. The general principles this skill shares with it (plain language, inverted pyramid, active voice, inclusive and trauma-informed writing, number literacy, EYD conventions) are widely published plain-language practice.

Authoring followed the guidance in anthropics/skills `skill-creator` (Apache-2.0); no files from it are included.
